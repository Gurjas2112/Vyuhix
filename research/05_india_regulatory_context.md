# 05 · India regulatory context

| Instrument | Key points for Vyuhix |
|---|---|
| **DPDP Act 2023** | Penalties up to ₹250 crore for failing to take reasonable security safeguards (Schedule) |
| **DPDP Rules 2025** (notified 13 Nov 2025) | Phased: Board constituted immediately; consent-manager provisions from 13 Nov 2026; substantive duties and Board penalties from **13 May 2027**. Breach: intimate the Board and affected people without delay; detailed report to the Board within **72 hours**. Minimum safeguards include encryption/masking/tokenisation, access control, logging and monitoring, backups, **one-year log retention**, and processor contracts |
| **CERT-In Directions (28 Apr 2022)** | Report specified incidents within **6 hours**; keep ICT logs for **180 days** in India; synchronise clocks |
| **IT Act 2000** | Unauthorised access is an offence: scanning must be authorised |
| **RBI IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Directions (2023)** | Control expectations for regulated lenders such as the demo's Acme Fintech |
| **SEBI CSCRF (2024)** | Cyber resilience framework for SEBI-regulated entities |
| **Insurance regulation** | Brokers and insurers are the regulated parties for advice and pricing; software shows analysis, not offers |

## How the product reflects this

- Regulatory loss is **date-aware** and can be toggled; the UI says enforcement begins in May 2027.
- The claims-readiness checklist carries the 6-hour and 72-hour clocks and log retention.
- The compliance matrix maps control areas to ISO 27001:2022, NIST CSF 2.0, CIS v8.1, CERT-In, RBI and DPDP.
- Insurance outputs carry a disclaimer and never show premiums; broker access requires client consent.
- Scanning is restricted to an allowlist in code.
